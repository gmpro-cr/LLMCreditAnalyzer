# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Relationship managers and credit officers at Indian commercial banks. They prepare and review Credit Appraisal Memorandums (CAMs) for corporate borrowers, usually against a sanction deadline, at a desk with the borrower's filings, the bank's templates and a credit committee waiting. The landing page (brand register) is the front door for credit teams evaluating the tool; the app behind it is where the RM builds the file.

## Product Purpose
CreditGuard drafts a full 21-section CAM for a listed borrower from public filings (Screener statements, BSE annual reports, peers, web research), with every figure traced to its source and the deterministic maths (ratios, DSCR, CMA, scorecard) kept separate from the language model's commentary. It lists what the RM still has to provide before sanction. Success is a credit officer trusting the draft enough to edit it rather than rewrite it.

## Brand Personality
Modern fintech, polished, exact. Confident without salesmanship: the product earns trust by showing its working (sources, audit trail, stated limits). Motion demonstrates the product doing its job rather than decorating.

## Anti-references
- Invented proof: customer logos, "80% faster", time-saved counters, fake testimonials. Every claim must be something the product actually does.
- Generic SaaS landing scaffolding: hero-metric blocks, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, eyebrow kickers over every section.
- Crypto/neo-bank glow aesthetics; dark-mode-by-default "AI" look.

## Design Principles
1. Show the working: sources, figures and limits are visible, not promised.
2. Document-first: the memorandum is the hero object; the UI frames it.
3. Exact over impressive: numbers are real (labelled), copy is specific.
4. Credit-committee calm: polish and motion that a bank's credit head would find credible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; full keyboard access; every animation has a prefers-reduced-motion alternative and content is never hidden behind a reveal.
